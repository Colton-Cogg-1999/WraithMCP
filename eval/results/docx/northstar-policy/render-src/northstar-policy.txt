Northstar Billing and Data Retention Policy
This policy explains how Northstar processes subscription payments through Northstar Pay and archives compliance records in LedgerVault.
1. Subscription Invoices and Grace Periods
Every Workspace receives an itemized monthly Invoice on the first business day of the billing cycle.
If a payment fails, we retry automatically for fourteen calendar days before pausing non-essential analytics exports.1
Review full tax documentation on the Northstar Help Center before submitting an exemption certificate.
2. Service Tiers and Retention Schedule
Plan Tier	Audit Log Retention	Support Response Target
Starter Workspace	Ninety days of searchable activity	Two business days via email
Enterprise Workspace	Seven years in LedgerVault	Four hours with priority escalation
3. Approved Legal Revisions
The compliance committee formally provisionally approved quarterly deletion audits for inactive merchant accounts.
Account owners may request immediate deletion of archived exports after settling every open Invoice.
Security notifications are delivered to the primary administrator and recorded in PulseGrid.
