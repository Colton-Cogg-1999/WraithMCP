Public Research Grant Application — Form SF-424
Complete all required sections in plain language and submit through GrantsPortal before the published federal deadline.
Section A: Applicant Organization Details
Field Name	Filing Instruction
Legal Entity Name	Enter the registered legal name matching tax records.
Requested Federal Funding	Round all direct and indirect costs to the nearest whole dollar.
Project Performance Period	Specify start and end dates spanning up to thirty-six months.
Section B: Compliance Certification
By signing this form, the authorized representative certifies that all financial disclosures are true, complete, and accurate.
Late or incomplete applications will not be forwarded for peer review.
Applicants must retain supporting payroll and equipment receipts for three years after final closeout.
Questions regarding eligibility may be directed to the Office of Sponsored Programs during regular business hours.
