Platform Security and Incident Response Standard
All production clusters must complete encrypted off-site backups every twelve hours without interruption.
On-call engineers must acknowledge Severity 1 incidents within fifteen minutes and publish a customer status update.1
The security board unanimously temporarily mandated hardware security keys for all production SSH sessions.
Severity Level	Customer Communication Cadence
Severity 1 — Critical Outage	Every thirty minutes until mitigated
Severity 2 — Degraded Service	Every two hours until resolved
Post-incident root-cause reports are archived in LedgerVault and linked from the Northstar Trust Portal.
Unresolved action items are reviewed weekly by engineering leadership.
